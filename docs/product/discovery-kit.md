# Discovery kit: accommodation operators, housekeeping turnover tracking

Prepared 2026-11-03 by the product-planner agent. Tailored to the segment recommended in
`docs/product/segment-comparison.md` (decision D1 is still open, due 2026-11-15). Adapts the
generic templates in `docs/PLANNING.md` section 9. All prices are **hypotheses**.

Privacy rule for this kit: prospect names, emails, phone numbers and property names never go
into this repository. Keep them in a private spreadsheet; refer to prospects here by id (P01 to P20).

## 1. Outreach message

Two variants, same content. Send one per prospect, personalised in the first line (something true
about their property: location, number of rooms, a review theme). No links, no attachments, no
follow-up more than once (after 5 days).

**Email / contact form**

> Subject: 4 quick questions about turnover cleaning at [property name]
>
> Hello [first name],
>
> I am building a simple tool that helps small guesthouses and rental hosts keep track of room
> turnovers between guests: which rooms need cleaning today, who is doing them, and whether they
> have been checked. Before building more, I am speaking with a few operators to understand what
> currently takes the most time or goes wrong.
>
> Would you be open to answering 4 short questions by email or WhatsApp? It takes about 10
> minutes, there is no sales call, and I will share what I learn from the other operators.
>
> Thank you,
> [founder name], FlexiManage

**Direct message (Facebook group, Airbnb Community Center, Instagram)**

> Hi [first name], I saw your [property / post about ...]. I am building a small tool for
> coordinating room turnovers between guests (who cleans what, by when, was it checked) and I am
> asking a few hosts 4 short questions about how they handle it today. By message, no call. Would
> you be up for it?

Response-rate hypothesis: 15 to 25 percent for personalised email to listed operators, higher in
communities where the founder has posted before. Target: 5 to 10 completed conversations from
20 messages by 2026-11-13.

## 2. Four-question script with follow-up prompts

Ask one question per message if the conversation is asynchronous. Do not pitch; do not mention
features until question 4 is answered.

**Q1. Tell me about the last change-over day: a guest left and another arrived. What did you and
your team do, step by step, from check-out to the room being ready?**
- Follow-ups: How did the cleaner know which rooms to do and in which order? How did you learn
  that a room was finished? Did anyone check the room before the next guest arrived?

**Q2. What do you use today to keep track of this? (Messages, a whiteboard, a spreadsheet, the
channel manager, a notebook, memory.)**
- Follow-ups: Could you send me a photo or screenshot of it, with guest names hidden? What do
  you write down and what stays in your head? Who else looks at it?

**Q3. Roughly how much time per week goes into coordinating cleaning and checks, and who spends
it?**
- Follow-ups: Is it you, a family member, a supervisor? How many rooms or units, how many
  turnovers in a busy week? Does the cleaner work for you or for a cleaning company?

**Q4. What happened the last time a room was missed, not ready, or not up to standard? How did you
find out?**
- Follow-ups: What did it cost you (refund, bad review, a late check-in)? What did you change
  afterwards? What would you want to know earlier next time?

Closing (only after Q4): "Thank you. I am setting up a small paid pilot in late November with
3 operators who want to try a simple shared task list for turnovers. Would you like me to send
the details?" Record yes / no / maybe.

## 3. Sourcing checklist for 20 prospects (channels, not individuals)

Target mix. Fill the quota per channel before moving to the next so the list is not all from one
source. Filter: 3 to 20 rooms or units, self-managed (host replies personally in reviews),
public email or messaging channel, active in the last 90 days.

| # | Channel | Quota | How to find them | Done |
|---|---------|------:|------------------|:----:|
| 1 | Booking.com and Airbnb listings in 2 or 3 chosen towns | 6 | Filter small properties; read host responses to reviews to confirm the owner manages; find the property's own website or Instagram for a direct contact | [ ] |
| 2 | Google Maps search "guesthouse", "B&B", "hostel", "apartments" in the same towns | 4 | Use the listed website or email; skip chains and franchise brands | [ ] |
| 3 | Local or regional tourism board accommodation directory | 3 | Often lists email addresses directly; prefer entries with 3 to 20 rooms | [ ] |
| 4 | Host communities: Airbnb Community Center, Facebook host groups, r/airbnb_hosts (read each group's rules on research posts first) | 3 | Reply to recent posts about cleaners, turnovers or missed cleans; message the poster | [ ] |
| 5 | Co-hosts and small property managers (5 to 20 units) found on Instagram or LinkedIn | 2 | Search "co-host", "short-term rental management" plus the town | [ ] |
| 6 | Personal network: anyone who has stayed at or knows a small guesthouse owner | 2 | Ask for an introduction by message; warm intros convert best | [ ] |

Also record, per prospect: id, channel, property size, contact method, date sent, date replied,
status (no reply / declined / answered / pilot interest). Keep this in the private spreadsheet.

Stop rules: if fewer than 3 conversations are completed by 2026-11-10, widen to a second region
or switch to the S1 fallback in `docs/product/segment-comparison.md`.

## 5. Pilot offer

Fixed scope, fixed price, 6 weeks, two success criteria agreed up front, two feedback points,
data export guaranteed. Dates count from a pilot start on **2026-11-17**.

**Offer text (send after Q4 to prospects who said yes or maybe)**

> FlexiManage housekeeping pilot, 2026-11-17 to 2026-12-29
>
> What you get: one private workspace for your property with a ready-made "Housekeeping tasks"
> list (room, due date, task type, status, assigned to, guests arriving, notes, inspected) set up
> with your rooms, up to 5 user logins for you and your cleaners, file attachments on tasks (photos
> of a finished room or damage), a CSV export of everything at any time, and support from me by
> message within one business day.
>
> What it does not include: bookings, a calendar, automatic task creation from your channel
> manager, or a mobile app. The web app works on a phone browser.
>
> Price: [49 USD one-time for the 6 weeks, hypothesis], paid on or before 2026-11-17. If you
> continue after the pilot, the Pro plan is [9 USD per month, hypothesis]. If you stop, you keep
> your CSV export and I delete your workspace on request.
>
> Two things we agree on now and check together: the two success criteria below. Feedback: a
> 20-minute message exchange or call on 2026-12-08 (week 3) and on 2026-12-29 (week 6).

**Scope (fixed)**

- One workspace, the "Housekeeping tasks" module from the template, select options edited to the
  property's real rooms. No second module during the pilot even if asked; note the request.
- Up to 5 members (Pro-plan hypothesis), roles owner for the operator, member for cleaners.
- Attachments, search, filter, sort, CSV export as specified in Epic B of `docs/PLANNING.md`.

**Price hypotheses to test** (label them as such in every conversation)

| Variant | Price | Test with |
|---------|-------|-----------|
| A | 49 USD one-time for 6 weeks, then Pro at 9 USD per month | Pilots 1 and 2 |
| B | Free for 6 weeks in exchange for both feedback sessions, then Pro at 9 USD per month | Only if two prospects decline variant A; the MVP success criteria need at least one paying pilot |

**Success criteria (defaults; agree two per pilot, adjust to their numbers)**

1. Coverage: in weeks 2 to 6 (2026-11-24 to 2026-12-29) at least 80 percent of the property's
   actual room turnovers exist as records with status Done, measured against the operator's
   booking calendar at the week-6 review.
2. Reliability: zero turnovers missed or late because of a coordination failure in weeks 4 to 6
   (2026-12-08 to 2026-12-29), as reported by the operator; baseline from Q4 of the interview.

Alternative criterion if the operator cares more about time: self-reported weekly coordination
time down at least 30 percent from the Q3 baseline by 2026-12-29.

**Timeline**

| Date | Event |
|------|-------|
| 2026-11-17 | Pilot start: workspace created, rooms configured, cleaners invited, first week's tasks entered together with the operator |
| 2026-11-24 | Measurement window for criterion 1 opens (week 2) |
| 2026-12-08 | Week-3 feedback: usage review, friction list, criterion 2 window opens |
| 2026-12-29 | Week-6 feedback: evaluate both criteria, pricing reaction, continue / stop decision |
| 2027-01-05 | CSV export delivered and workspace deletion on request completed; outcome recorded in `docs/PLANNING.md` Phase 6 |

**Timing caveat for the project lead:** the roadmap has the usable product at milestone M3
(2026-12-26) and pilot onboarding in Phase 6 (from 2027-01-16). A 2026-11-17 start therefore
assumes an early-access pilot on the staging build while Epic B lands, with the founder entering
the first weeks' tasks by hand if needed. Either accept that (and treat the pilot as discovery
plus validation) or shift every date above by the same offset when the start is fixed.
